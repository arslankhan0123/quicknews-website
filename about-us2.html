About us

Quick News Global (AI) is built on a simple belief, staying informed shouldn't require hours of scrolling, endless news articles, or falling into clickbait traps. In a world flooded with information, misinformation, manufactured urgency, and competing narratives, we use AI to help people understand what truly matters. We decode the News. For every story, our AI determines whether it's worth your attention, who is affected, what the real issue is, what the likely consequences may be, and how it fits into the broader historical context. We also transform this information into a 45-word brief that can be read in as little as 9 seconds, enhanced with AI-powered sentiment analysis so you can understand not only what is being reported, but how and why it is being framed.

Our Mission

Our mission is to empower every individual with fast, meaningful news intelligence. Using AI we transform every headline into instant clarity to tell you who is affected, why it matters, and whether you need to read further, all in under 9 seconds, so you can spend less time scrolling and more time with your family, community and the things that truly matter. Because the best informed people aren't the ones reading everything, they're the ones who know what truly matters.
