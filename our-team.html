Ali (Founder)

For me Quick News Global (AI) was never just a technology project, it is deeply personal. My own journey, shaped by hardship and a constant struggle for knowledge, taught me something that never left me, information changes lives. When people truly understand the world around them, they make better decisions, build stronger communities and live with greater purpose.

That conviction led to one question that kept us up at night, in a world flooded with news, why do so many people feel more confused than informed? The answer was not more content. It was smarter content. Faster intelligence. Cleaner clarity. So we built something genuinely different. Quick News Global (AI) analyses every headline and in under 9 seconds tells you exactly what you need to know. What we do is very simple: Should you read this story, who is affected, what the real issue is beneath the headline, and the historical context that makes it all make sense. Our AI then generates a 45-word intelligence brief that gives you the full picture before you decide whether to read further.

We are a news intelligence platform. We do not tell you what to think, we give you everything you need to think for yourself, clearly, quickly and without bias.

We are a bootstrapped team, each with full-time careers and building this from the ground up because we believe in it that deeply. We launched in the USA but our vision reaches every country, every culture and every individual on the planet. Because when people are truly informed, they are stronger together.

This is just the beginning. Join us in building a more informed, connected and understanding world.
